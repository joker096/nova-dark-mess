Mess&Anger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

The short version

Mess&Anger is an end-to-end encrypted peer-to-peer (P2P) messenger. You do not create an account: your identity is a cryptographic keypair generated on your device. We never see your message content. Moderation uses pseudonymous reports; ads in public channels use contextual placement and aggregate statistics, never an advertising profile.

How the app works

  • Messages are end-to-end encrypted. Content is encrypted on your device with X25519 ECDH + Ed25519 signatures. The relay server only ever sees encrypted blobs — it cannot read your messages, contacts, or files.
  • No account, no email, no phone number. Your identity is a local recovery phrase / master seed stored only on your device. The server recognizes you solely by your Ed25519 public key (a pseudonymous identifier).

What we collect

  • Connection record. Each time your device connects to the relay: your device public key, IP address, user agent string, country, and connect/disconnect timestamps. Used to operate and secure the connection service.
  • Moderation reports. A report contains the target identifier (a post, comment, channel or campaign), a reason code, an optional note and a case id. The note is encrypted to the moderation key when it leaves the device. To limit spam, the moderation service may attach a rotating reporter token that is not linked to your identity, IP address or contacts. Moderators see the reported content and case history, not your encrypted chats.
  • Sanctions. A sanction is recorded against the pseudonymous fingerprint of the reported identity, with a rule id, level, reason, issue time and expiry. Signed ban-list entries are distributed to relays and clients so they can enforce the decision.
  • In-app ad events. Mess&Anger shows first-party ads only in public channels, with no third-party advertising SDK. Impressions and clicks are counted with short-lived blinded tokens and local aggregation. We do not store an advertising ID, device ID, IP address or user key with an ad event. Channel owners and advertisers receive aggregate counts, revenue and fill rate only, with a minimum group size of 100 and a 24-hour delay.
  • Payment records (premium and ad credits). Premium purchases are processed by a third-party payment gateway (paymento.io). We store order metadata: order ID, amount, currency, status, timestamp and the device public key needed to grant and verify entitlements. Ad credits and channel payouts use prepaid codes, crypto invoices or a payment link; we store campaign id, amount, currency, status and a pseudonymous wallet reference. Card details are handled exclusively by the payment gateway; we never see your full payment details.

What we do NOT collect

  • Message content, attachments, or media — encrypted end-to-end, readable only by the participants.
  • Email address, phone number, or real name.
  • Contact lists — stored only on your device.
  • Third-party analytics, tracking pixels, advertising IDs, cross-site profiles, or advertising SDKs. The app contains no external analytics or tracking code.
  • Cookies and tracking pixels. Sessions are device-local.
  • Passport, legal-entity, tax or banking documents from users, channel owners or advertisers. Nova does not run KYC for ads.

What stays on your device

Your identity (recovery phrase / master seed), contacts, chat history, and media cache are stored locally in your browser / app storage. This data never leaves your device except when you send it (encrypted) to another participant, or when you use a payment feature.

Data retention and deletion

Connection logs are kept while we operate the service. Ad events are aggregated and stripped of identifiers before storage; raw events are discarded. Reports and appeal records are kept for up to 12 months after the case closes, then deleted or aggregated. Payment order records are kept as required to support subscriptions and for legitimate accounting.

You may request access to, or deletion of, any data stored against your device public key. Because your identity is a public key you hold locally, you can also permanently leave the service by generating a fresh identity on your device — your old key simply stops connecting.

Your rights

  • Access: request the data we hold for your device key.
  • Correction: no personal attributes to correct beyond connection metadata.
  • Deletion: request removal of connection logs, ad events, or payment records, and delete the local moderation outbox from the app.
  • Opt-out of ads: hide individual ads, block advertisers on your device, or subscribe to Premium to remove ads.
  • Appeal: appeal a sanction once in the app without providing an email address.

Third parties

  • paymento.io — payment processor for premium subscriptions. Subject to their own privacy policy; they receive only what is required to process a payment.
  • Hosting — the relay server is hosted by our provider; connection metadata is stored there under our control.
  • Ad payment and payout rails — prepaid codes, crypto invoices or a payment link. They may process their own compliance data, but Nova does not receive identity documents or KYC files.
  • No advertising networks, no analytics vendors, no data brokers, no ad-profile resellers.

Anonymous mode (experimental)

The optional anonymous mode uses one-way relay queues with fixed-size sealed packets. A relay stores ciphertext only until the recipient acknowledges it or the queue TTL expires. With a proxy configured, the relay sees the proxy address instead of yours; with Tor configured, traffic leaves through a local Tor SOCKS listener to a .onion relay. This mode is experimental and has not been independently audited.

Residual risks you should know about:

  • a passive observer can correlate send and poll timing despite padding and jitter;
  • the direct relay path without a proxy or Tor reveals your IP address to the relay;
  • an invite can be used once by whoever reads it, and sealed sender does not prove who sent a message;
  • if push hints are enabled, the push provider learns that a notification was delivered;
  • a device compromise bypasses all of the above.

Website chat widget

Website owners can install the free Mess&Anger chat widget (see Widget documentation). This section describes what the widget means for you as a visitor of such a site:

  • No cookies, no third-party trackers. The widget sets no cookies on the site's domain and loads no fonts, scripts, pixels or analytics from any third party. All code is first-party.
  • Encryption keys stay in your browser. The conversation key is created in the visitor's browser and transported only in the URL fragment (#), which is never sent to the server, never stored in page content and never written to our logs.
  • The relay sees ciphertext only. Messages are end-to-end encrypted before they leave the visitor's device; the relay cannot read them, and undelivered messages are deleted with the queue.
  • Handoff to the app. If the site owner uses handoff, you can continue the conversation in the Mess&Anger app via a one-time link; the conversation is shown only in your app, and the site does not gain access to it. You may cancel at any time.
  • Aggregate site statistics. The site owner sees only anonymous, aggregate counters (widget shown, opened, first message sent, owner answered) — never your identity, IP address or conversation content.
  • Abuse protection. Guest sessions are rate-limited and proof-of-work protected; the site owner can block a guest session from abusive use.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
