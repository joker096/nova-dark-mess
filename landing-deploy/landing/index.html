Every feature ships with privacy and security baked in. No toggle to turn off — because you shouldn't have to choose between usability and anonymity.

01 · P2P Network

Direct device-to-device connection

WebRTC creates an encrypted tunnel directly between you and your contact. No server ever sees your messages — there's nothing in between.

02 · Cryptography

X3DH + Double Ratchet

Signal-style E2EE: X25519/Ed25519 key agreement, a fresh key per message and AES-256-GCM at rest. The protocol spec is open for review.

03 · Anonymity

No phone or email required

Identity is your cryptographic fingerprint (a BIP-39 seed you back up). Add contacts via QR code, username, or nova:// link.

Security architecture

Defense
in depth

From RFC 5869 HKDF to per-message ratchet keys. Each layer is independent — if one is compromised, the rest hold. Full protocol spec in the repository.

Anti-censorship

Hard
to block

Several transports with automatic fallback. Not magic: a censor who blocks every endpoint can still cut access — which is why you can run and register your own relay.

Direct WebRTC

Standard P2P. Direct encrypted tunnel with no intermediaries.

WebSocket tunnel

HTTP/SOCKS5 proxy with MTProto obfuscation.

MTProto obfuscation

Fake TLS headers, XOR, random padding, timing jitter.

Self-hosted relays

Run your own signaling/relay gateway so blocking one domain does not break the network.

Auto-switching

WebRTC → WS tunnel → obfuscated transport. Automatic.

FAQ

Frequently
asked questions

What happens if I lose my phone?
If you created an encrypted backup (.nova-backup file) or saved your 24-word recovery phrase — restore your account on a new device. Without one of them, data is lost forever.
Can the government block Mess&Anger?
Hard, not impossible. Traffic can run over WebRTC, a WebSocket tunnel or obfuscated transports, and you can add your own relay — but a sufficiently resourced censor can still block the app's endpoints.
Can someone read my messages?
Only you and the recipient. Messages are encrypted on your device and decrypted only on the recipient's device.
Is the encryption post-quantum?
Not yet. Nova uses the classical Signal stack (X3DH + Double Ratchet). A hybrid post-quantum handshake is on the roadmap; Signal made the same transition with PQXDH.
How do I add a contact?
Scan a QR code, find by username, or get a nova:// link. There is no central address book.
Is Stars a cryptocurrency?
No. Stars is a local reward system for activity. No blockchain, no market value.
